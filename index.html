Your data stays with you. What you type into the budget tracker is saved only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody else who opens these pages can see it. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use "Save a copy with my data" or "Download backup" now and then. Your 5-year plan lives in your own Claude chat.

On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to open these like an app. These are planning tools, not financial advice.
