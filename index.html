Money Tools

Two free tools for paying down debt and building savings. They work on phones and computers, no account needed.

Budget trackerAdd the CSV files from your bank and card websites and see your spending by category against your plan, by month or by paycheck. Five-year money planA worksheet for your monthly plan, loan payoff date, savings goals and to-do list.

Your data stays with you. Everything you enter is saved only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody else who opens these pages can see it. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use "Save a copy with my data" or "Download backup" now and then.

On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to open these like an app. These are planning tools, not financial advice.
