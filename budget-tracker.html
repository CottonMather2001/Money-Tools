  • Save to Claude: copies your data with a short note and opens Claude. Paste it and send. How to use

  1. Open Set up your plan and replace the example amounts with your own monthly spending plan and loan and savings targets.
  2. On your bank and card websites, download recent transactions as CSV. Choose CSV, not PDF or Quicken.
  3. Tap Add CSV files. You can add several files at once, and adding the same file twice won't double count.
  4. Tap a category to see its charges and its monthly amount. Use the dropdown on any charge to move it to another category.
  5. Add your own rules for things only you would know: your employer, landlord, loan servicer, or a person you pay regularly.
  6. Use Bi-weekly to see spending by paycheck. Pay periods start on each paycheck deposit, and each plan amount becomes the monthly amount × 12 ÷ 26.
  7. Tap Ask Claude about my spending to send your numbers to your own Claude chat for analysis (a free account works).
  8. Repeat each month. When you're done, tap Save a copy with my data and open that newest file next time. That way your data never depends on the browser remembering it.

Card payments and transfers between your own accounts are left out of spending so nothing is counted twice. This is a tracking tool, not financial advice.

Ask Claude about your spending

Pick a question, or write your own at the top of the box. The button copies your numbers and the question, then opens Claude in a new tab, where a free account works. If the message box there is empty, paste. Your numbers only go to your own Claude chat.
